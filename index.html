(01) About the show

We center the athlete, not the soundbite.

The Ananya Subramanian Show is an interview-only podcast. Every episode is a long-form sit-down of around forty minutes, recorded virtually, with the players, coaches, rising talent, creators and owners who make world sport what it is. No hot takes and no highlight reels: just the person, in their own words.

  1. 01

    The sit-down

    One guest, about forty minutes, no rush. Room for the questions a press conference never gets to.

  2. 02

    World sports

    It started in cricket as Yorker. Now it's every sport: the pitch, the court, the track and anywhere else a story is waiting.

  3. 03

    The whole game

    The early mornings, the setbacks, the second chances and the people behind them. The whole career, not just the result.

Conversations that go past the scoreline into the person.
